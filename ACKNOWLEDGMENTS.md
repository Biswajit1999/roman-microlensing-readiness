## Data and methods

- The injection-recovery benchmark is built on the public **2018 WFIRST
  (now Roman) Microlensing Data Challenge** dataset, simulated by Matthew
  Penny and collaborators and distributed at
  <https://github.com/microlensing-data-challenge/data-challenge-1>. See
  `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md` for full provenance and access details.
- Roman Galactic Bulge Time Domain Survey (GBTDS) design parameters follow
  the community-standard reference of Penny et al. (2019, ApJS 241, 3) and
  Johnson et al. (2020, AJ 160, 123); see `docs/METHODS.md` and
  `CITATION.cff`.
- This project is an independent research benchmark and is not an official
  Roman Space Telescope project product, and is not affiliated with or
  endorsed by NASA, STScI, or the RGES Project Infrastructure Team.
